# Versioning namespaces

Prismora previously used `v0.2` for two distinct artifacts. To preserve history without ambiguity, versions are now qualified by product namespace:

- `toolkit-v0.1.x`: founding data-collection and analysis toolkit.
- `toolkit-v0.2.0`: hardened toolkit produced in the Claude/Opus workstream.
- `lab-v0.2.0`: unified Prismora J-Lens Lab release.
- `lab-v0.2.1`: Human Visualizer patch on top of the lab.
- `lab-v0.3.0-buildweek`: planned Build Week tranche.

Existing archive filenames are not rewritten. New documentation, tags, and releases should use the qualified names.
