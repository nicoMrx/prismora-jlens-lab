# Provenance

Prismora is an existing independent research project directed by Julie / NicoMrx.

- The founding v0.1 toolkit and its core invariants—immutable raw responses, request hashing, resumable runs, and separate normalization—were developed collaboratively with Claude and Opus.
- Prismora J-Lens Lab v0.2.0—the unified protocol engine, web laboratory, Neuronpedia adapter, reproducible storage, and initial private-GPU worker contract—was developed with GPT-5.6 Pro.
- The Human Visualizer v0.2.1 was added afterward to make archived J-Lens observations directly inspectable by the human researcher rather than only through raw JSON.
- Pepper, Opus, and Fable were used as cross-checking research and review tools. Julie selected the questions, supervised the work, made the experimental decisions, and retains responsibility for the claims and publication.
- The `build-week-2026` branch is reserved for the submission-specific tranche implemented with Codex and GPT-5.6. Pre-existing work and Build Week work must remain distinguishable in Git history and submission materials.

This record describes collaboration roles; it does not treat language models as authors or independent scientific observers.
